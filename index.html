Computational PathologyM.S. Thesis

CellSeg-UNICls

Decoupled cell instance segmentation and embedding-based cell classification for brain histopathology.

0.57 → 0.75 macro F1 after replacing the classifier, with Cellpose-SAM segmentation held fixed.
Problem
Joint segmentation-and-classification models were limited by segmentation quality, and expert labels existed only as points.
Data
6 patients · 48 image patches · 13,697 annotated instances.
Limitation
Small dataset with no external validation cohort yet.

Pipeline
01Expert annotationPoint labels converted to instance masks with the QuPath SAM extension.
02Instance segmentationCellpose-SAM detects and outlines each cell.
03Embedding classificationFrozen UNI2 embeddings feed a separate MLP classifier.

My contribution: I converted expert point annotations into instance masks, revised them over several rounds of expert review, and built the pipeline that separates segmentation from classification.
